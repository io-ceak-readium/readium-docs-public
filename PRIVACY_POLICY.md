# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-21

Readium (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 User Content (Stored Locally)
The App allows users to create and store:

- Reading records
- Reading sessions
- Notes and memos
- Timeline history

This data is stored locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to our servers.

---

### 1.2 Anonymous Authentication (Firebase)

The App uses **Google Firebase Authentication (Anonymous Sign-In)**.

Firebase may process technical information such as:

- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Authentication tokens

This processing is handled by Google under their own privacy policies.

---

### 1.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect:

- Advertising ID
- Device information
- Usage data
- Network information
- Diagnostic information

This information is used to provide and improve advertising services.

For more information, please refer to:
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Store and manage your reading records locally
- Authenticate users anonymously
- Display advertisements
- Improve app stability and performance

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

However, the App uses third-party services:

- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

---

## 4. Data Retention

- Reading data is stored locally on your device.
- If you uninstall the App, local data is removed.
- Firebase anonymous authentication data is managed by Google.

---

## 5. Data Security

Data transmitted through Firebase and AdMob is encrypted using secure HTTPS connections.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 7. Your Rights

You may:

- Delete local data by uninstalling the App
- Reset your device advertising ID via system settings
