# Library

Library is where your books live.

---

## Book status

A book can have one of these states:

- **Reading**
- **Completed**
- **Stopped**

---

## Mark as completed

When you mark a book as completed:

- an active session (if any) is ended automatically
- a completion entry appears in the timeline

Notes are optional.

---

## Delete a book

Deleting a book removes its related logs and notes as well.
