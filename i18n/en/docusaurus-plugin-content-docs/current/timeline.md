# Timeline

Timeline is the center of Readium.

Your reading doesn’t scatter — it becomes a chronological flow.

---

## What gets recorded?

- session start
- session end
- completion
- notes

Everything is placed in time order.

---

## Why it matters

Timeline is not just a log.

It helps you:

- see your reading pattern
- find where you stopped
- continue again with context
